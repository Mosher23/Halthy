THE NEXT CHAPTER

A calmer look. A clearer everyday.

A more consistent design, simpler Settings and thoughtful refinements to the way you explore, export and connect your data.

Design

One look, throughout the app

  • A shared icon style brings subtle fills and clear, colourful outlines to the dashboard, Settings, setup and workout replay.
  • Compact status popovers make connection and upload details easier to read. Clearer outlines also respond to Increase Contrast.

Improved

Less friction. More flow.

  • Settings is organised into Connections, Health & data, and Preferences, with compact grouped rows and clearer connection feedback.
  • Open Export directly from Settings. Setup screens and replay controls now share the same visual language, and workout icons reflect the actual activity.

Refined

The details matter

  • Technical log details expand inline, keeping repeated events compact while retaining filters and CSV sharing.
  • Further improvements to workout-upload recovery and sleep time-in-bed handling make recorded data and connection problems easier to understand.

Planned for October 30. App Store availability follows release and may vary by region.

1.1October 26, 2026Feature update

THE STORY SO FAR

A fresh perspective on your health.

Refreshed themes, richer workout replay and more control over the health data you choose to explore and share.

New look

A dashboard that feels like you

  • Redesigned light and dark themes, patterned backgrounds and clearer dashboard controls.
  • Recorded sleep stages with durations and percentages when available, plus easier access to Dietary Energy in health-data selection.

Workouts

Revisit the effort

  • Improved workout replay, satellite-map readability and weather overlays.
  • Reorganised Settings and setup, including Home Assistant sign-in and Digital Health configuration for eligible users.

Reliability

Clearer feedback. Smoother sync.

  • Improved background upload handling for HealthKit, eligible Digital Health readings and InfluxDB, with additional sync and stability improvements.
  • More readable technical logs with timestamped CSV export and system sharing, clearer connection-test feedback and more reliable status-badge outlines.

Background uploads depend on iOS scheduling and data availability. Digital Health requires an eligible EU device and Apple Account, iOS 26.4 or later, and separate permission.

1.0September 24, 2026The first release

THE STORY SO FAR

Your workouts. Your health. Your data.

The beginning of Halthy: a personal view of Apple Health, with tools to revisit your workouts and take selected data wherever you want it to go.

Explore

Your daily perspective

  • A customisable health dashboard with selected metrics, charts, trends and sleep summaries from Apple Health.
  • A workout calendar, route maps and replay, with shareable workout images and videos when the required workout data is available.

Export & connect

Always on your terms

  • Local CSV and PDF exports, plus GPX files for recorded workout routes.
  • Optional connections to your own Home Assistant and InfluxDB, without a Halthy account or Halthy-operated health-data server.

The 1.0.1 maintenance update also arrived on September 24, improving interface performance, iPhone layouts, queued uploads and general stability.
